Calibrate your printer

Printers are often slightly off: lines come out a bit too long, the edges stay blank, or the back doesn't line up with the front. Print one test sheet, read five numbers from it and Quire corrects your prints. You only need to do this once per printer. The values are saved on this device and apply to all notebooks.

Print it like your notebooks: plain A4, scale 100 % / Actual size, margins None, no headers and footers, both sides, flipped on the long edge. Not “Fit to page” and not borderless. The arrows on front and back must point the same way. The numbers below match the ones on the sheet.

1Size

Measure the long horizontal and the long vertical line with a ruler, from the middle of one end tick to the middle of the other. Using the same edge of both ticks is easiest: left edge to left edge, top edge to top edge. Both lines should be 160 mm. Enter what you measure, as exactly as you can.

mm
mm

A value that is more than 1.5 % off isn't corrected. The print dialog probably scaled the sheet: print it again at 100 %.

2Edges

Each edge has a row of numbered lines, and the ones your printer can't reach are missing. On all four edges of both pages, find the smallest number whose line is printed. Enter the largest of these eight numbers. In the picture, that edge reads 3.5.

mm

Use the printed number, not the distance to the paper edge. If the two differ, the paper isn't where the printer expects it. On double-sided prints that pushes front and back apart, which step 3 corrects, but it costs usable space. If it's more than about 2 mm, first check the paper guides and that the paper size is set to A4, then print the sheet again.

3Front and back

Hold the sheet against the light with the front facing you. The red lines from the back show through. Read where they cross the two scales in the middle of the sheet, including the sign. The picture reads +1.5 across and −1 down. Quire moves front and back by half of it each, so they meet.

mm
mm

If the two skew checks show clearly different values, the paper goes in crooked, which Quire can't correct. The offset also varies a little from sheet to sheet, so it helps to print a few and enter the average.

Check the result

Print the sheet again, now with your correction. The lines should be 160 mm and the red lines should cross at 0. Don't enter values from this sheet: only the uncorrected one counts.
