What stays on your device

Your settings, printer calibration and saved notebooks, including cover images and texts, are stored only in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB). They aren't sent to the server. You can delete them at any time by clearing the data of this site in your browser.

Images you upload from your device never leave your browser.

Visiting the site

The site runs on a server hosted by netcup GmbH, Germany. When you open a page, the server necessarily receives your IP address and the usual details of a request, such as the page and your browser, to send you the page (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Requests are not logged. Only technical errors may be, and such an entry can contain an IP address.

Images from the web

If you paste or drop the address of an image, your browser loads it directly from the server it's on, and again whenever you open Quire with that image. That server then sees your IP address.

If you use a link to a Pinterest pin, Quire asks Pinterest for the image and, if that fails, the public proxy api.allorigins.win. Both see your IP address and the link, and may process them outside the EU. This only happens when you load such a link yourself.
